Day 2: Diagnosing Hardware Failures

Task 1: Five Common Hardware Issues and Their Symptoms

No.| Hardware Issue| Typical Symptom
1| No display on monitor| Blank screen or no display signal.
2| Faulty RAM| Computer crashes, freezes, or restarts unexpectedly.
3| Hard drive failure| Slow performance or files not opening.
4| Faulty cooling fan| Computer overheats and may shut down.
5| Power supply failure| Computer does not turn on or shuts down unexpectedly.

Task 2: Troubleshooting Plan for a Laptop That Is Not Charging

Problem: A laptop is not charging even when plugged in.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Plan:

1. Check the power outlet: Make sure the wall socket is working properly.
2. Check the charger: Inspect the charging adapter and cable for visible damage.
3. Check the charging port: Look for dust or visible damage.
4. Check the power connection: Make sure the charger is properly connected to the laptop.
5. Check the battery indicator: Observe whether the charging light turns on.
6. Restart the laptop: Restart the laptop and check whether charging starts.

Conclusion: These basic checks can help identify common charging problems. If the problem continues, consult a qualified technician.

Task 4: Checking the 12V Rail of a Power Supply

Problem: A PC is randomly shutting down.

Objective: To understand how the 12V rail of a power supply is evaluated.

Expected Voltage: 12V

Acceptable Range: Approximately 11.4V to 12.6V.

Steps:

1. Identify the 12V rail of the ATX power supply.
2. Understand that the yellow wire represents +12V and the black wire represents ground.
3. Use a virtual multimeter simulator or observe a teacher's supervised demonstration.
4. Compare the displayed voltage with the expected 12V range.
5. Record the result and identify whether the voltage is within the acceptable range.

Example Simulated Result: 12.1V

Conclusion: A voltage below 11.5V may indicate a power supply problem and could contribute to unexpected shutdowns.

Safety Note: Do not open or physically probe a power supply. Electrical testing must be performed by a qualified technician or under direct teacher supervision.
