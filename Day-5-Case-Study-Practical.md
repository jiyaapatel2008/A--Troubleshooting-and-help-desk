Day 5: Case Study Practical – Multiple Hardware Issues

Task 1: Laptop Does Not Power On

Scenario: A user reports that their laptop does not power on even when plugged in.

Five Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Check the Power Adapter: Make sure the charger is properly connected and working.
2. Check the Power Outlet: Try another power socket to confirm that the outlet is working.
3. Check the Battery: Check whether the battery is charging or completely drained.
4. Perform a Hard Reset: Disconnect the charger and hold the power button for 15–20 seconds. Reconnect the charger and try again.
5. Check Hardware Connections: Check for loose or damaged power connectors and other hardware issues.

Conclusion: These steps help identify whether the problem is related to the charger, battery, power supply, or internal hardware.

Task 2: Monitor Shows a Blank Screen

Scenario: The monitor shows a blank screen, but the CPU seems to be running.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Flow:

1. Check whether the monitor is switched on.
2. Check the power cable of the monitor.
3. Check whether the HDMI or DisplayPort cable is connected properly.
4. Select the correct input source on the monitor.
5. Check the graphics card connection.
6. Test the monitor with another computer if possible.
7. Restart the computer and check whether the display returns.

Conclusion: The problem may be caused by a loose cable, incorrect input source, faulty monitor, or graphics card issue.

Task 3: Wireless Mouse Is Not Working

Scenario: A user complains that their wireless mouse is unresponsive, but the keyboard works fine.

How to Identify the Problem:

1. Check the Mouse:

* Check whether the mouse is switched on.
* Replace or recharge the batteries.
* Check whether the mouse sensor is clean.
* Reconnect the wireless receiver.

2. Check the USB Port:

* Remove the USB receiver.
* Connect it to another USB port.
* Check whether the USB port is working properly.

3. Check System Settings:

* Check the mouse settings.
* Update or reinstall the mouse driver.
* Restart the computer.

Conclusion: These steps help identify whether the issue is with the mouse, USB port, or system settings.

Task 4: Gaming PC Overheating and Shutting Down

Scenario: A gaming PC is overheating and shutting down during use.

Three Possible Hardware Causes and Solutions:

1. CPU Overheating:

* Cause: Dust buildup or a faulty CPU cooling fan.
* Solution: Clean the CPU cooler and check the fan. Ensure proper airflow.

2. GPU Overheating:

* Cause: Dust buildup or improper GPU cooling.
* Solution: Clean the GPU fans and check the GPU temperature.

3. Faulty Power Supply (PSU):

* Cause: An insufficient or faulty power supply can cause system instability and unexpected shutdowns.
* Solution: Check the PSU connections and test with a compatible, properly rated power supply.

Conclusion: Regular cleaning, proper cooling, and hardware maintenance help prevent overheating and unexpected shutdowns.
